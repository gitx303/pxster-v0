Data deletion instructions

DRAFT — not effective. Business details and operator review are required before launch.

Disconnect a social account

Sign in to Pxster, open Connected accounts, and disconnect the relevant account. This removes the provider credentials stored by Pxster. To revoke the application's grant completely, also remove Pxster from that platform's connected-app settings. For Google, use Google account permissions.

Request deletion of Pxster data

Contact [MONITORED DELETION EMAIL REQUIRED] from the email associated with your Pxster account. Use the subject “Pxster data deletion” and state whether you want your whole account or specific content removed. Include your username and the scope of your request; never include passwords, session cookies, or platform tokens.

The operator must verify account ownership before acting. Deletion must cover the requested database records and R2 media, remove provider credentials and sessions, and stop pending publishing work. Pxster currently has no self-service account-deletion endpoint; this is an operator-managed process.

What deletion does not remove

Posts already published to Facebook, TikTok, or YouTube must be deleted on those services. Disconnecting alone does not delete your Pxster uploads, post history, or already-published content.

Timing and confirmation

Before launch: the operator must confirm a monitored contact, response and completion times, any required retention exceptions, backup expiry, and a confirmation process. No deletion deadline is promised by this draft.

This is a human-readable instructions URL, not a Facebook signed-request deletion callback. Do not configure it as an automated callback endpoint.
