Data deletion instructions

Pre-launch draft — X303 operates in the Philippines. Contact inboxes are awaiting setup and monitoring; deletion and backup timelines remain under review. These pages are not yet effective.

Contact setup pending: The addresses below are designated for Pxster but are not yet confirmed as receiving or monitored inboxes. Do not rely on them to submit time-sensitive requests until this notice is removed after testing.

Contact X303

To request deletion of data held by Pxster, email privacy@pxster.xyz with the subject Pxster data deletion. X303 operates Pxster in the Philippines and will handle these requests once the privacy inbox is active.

What to include

Send the request from the email address associated with your Pxster account. Include your username and say whether you want your entire account deleted or only particular uploads or connected-platform data removed. Identify the relevant platform or content when requesting a partial deletion.

Never include passwords, session cookies, authorization tokens, or copies of identity documents in your initial message. If you cannot access your registered email, explain that in your request so account ownership can be checked before any data is disclosed or deleted.

How requests are handled

Account deletion is an operator-managed process; Pxster does not currently offer a self-service account deletion button. The process requires verifying ownership, stopping pending publishing work, revoking Pxster sessions, removing stored platform credentials, and deleting the requested account records and stored media. A partial deletion may require clarification if other scheduled posts still use that media.

A deletion request email does not immediately stop scheduled publishing. If you need to stop further sends while your request is reviewed, disconnect the affected accounts in Pxster and revoke access with the platform. A publication already in progress may still complete.

Disconnecting a platform account

Sign in, open Connected accounts, and disconnect the relevant account. This removes the authorization tokens stored by Pxster, while retaining the account record and publishing history. Also remove Pxster in the platform's connected-app settings to revoke its grant. Google users can visit Google account permissions.

Published posts and third-party data

Deleting Pxster data does not remove posts already sent to Facebook, TikTok, or YouTube. Delete those posts directly on the respective platform. Each platform controls its own records under its privacy policy.

Timing, backups, and confirmation

Proposed timelines for review are acknowledgement within 7 days, completion of verified requests within 30 days, and expiry of backup copies within 90 days. These are placeholders, not active service commitments. X303 must review applicable requirements, confirm operational capability and retention exceptions, and approve a confirmation process before these instructions take effect.

For privacy concerns or questions about a request, contact privacy@pxster.xyz. For general account support, contact hey@pxster.xyz. See the privacy policy for related information.
