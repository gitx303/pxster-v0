Privacy policy

Pre-launch draft — X303 operates in the Philippines. Contact inboxes are awaiting setup and monitoring; deletion and backup timelines remain under review. These pages are not yet effective.

Contact setup pending: The addresses below are designated for Pxster but are not yet confirmed as receiving or monitored inboxes. Do not rely on them to submit time-sensitive requests until this notice is removed after testing.

Who operates Pxster

Pxster is operated by X303 in the Philippines. For privacy questions, access or correction requests, and data deletion, contact privacy@pxster.xyz. For general support, contact hey@pxster.xyz.

Information used by the service

When you create an account, Pxster stores your username, email address, password hash, and login session records. When you connect a social account, it stores platform account and channel identifiers, permissions, encrypted authorization tokens, and token expiry information.

Pxster also stores uploaded media and its validation results, captions, selected destinations, schedules, publishing states, platform post identifiers, and publishing attempt and error records. Support correspondence may contain the information you send us. Hosting services may process IP addresses and connection and security logs.

Why information is processed

Pxster uses this information to authenticate you, connect the accounts you authorize, validate media, carry out your publishing instructions, show results and history, troubleshoot failures, answer requests, and protect the service against abuse. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in support messages unless it is necessary to resolve your request.

Who receives information

When you publish or schedule content, the content and information needed to fulfill that instruction are sent to the platforms you select. Those platforms handle information under their own terms and privacy policies. Publicly published content may be visible to anyone.

Cloudflare provides website delivery, network protection, and media storage for the production service. The backend hosting service processes account and publishing records. Zoho Mail handles correspondence sent to our contact addresses. Service providers may process information outside your country. Production hosting locations and retention arrangements remain undecided and must be confirmed before launch.

Google and YouTube

Pxster uses YouTube API Services. Connecting YouTube allows Pxster to identify your authorized channel, upload the content you select, and retrieve publishing status. Pxster's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See the Google Privacy Policy.

You can disconnect YouTube in Pxster and revoke authorization through your Google account permissions. Revocation and account disconnection are distinct from deleting data already stored by Pxster. To request that deletion, follow our data deletion instructions.

Cookies and security

Pxster uses an essential session cookie to keep you signed in. Sessions expire after seven days, and signing out revokes the current session. Production cookies are configured with Secure, HttpOnly, and SameSite protections. Passwords are hashed and stored platform tokens are encrypted. These measures do not make every database field encrypted or eliminate all security risks.

Media delivery links provide temporary access to anyone who has the link so that your selected platforms can retrieve an upload. Treat these links as private and avoid sharing them.

Retention

The current service retains account information, uploads, and publishing history until they are manually removed. Disconnecting a social account clears its stored authorization tokens but retains the account record and publishing history. There is no automatic media retention or self-service account deletion feature.

Before this policy takes effect, X303 must confirm retention limits, deletion response times, and how deletion applies to backups. The proposed 7-day acknowledgement, 30-day verified deletion, and 90-day backup expiry periods are placeholders for review, not active commitments or automated controls.

Your choices and requests

You can choose which accounts to connect and where to publish, disconnect a platform account, and revoke access in that platform's settings. Email privacy@pxster.xyz to request access, correction, or deletion, or raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify account ownership before disclosing or changing information. Do not send your password, session cookies, or platform authorization tokens.

Changes and contact

Updates will be published on this page with a revision date. Material changes to how personal information is handled should be communicated before taking effect. Privacy requests go to privacy@pxster.xyz; general support goes to hey@pxster.xyz.
