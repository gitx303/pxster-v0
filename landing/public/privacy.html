Information used by the service

Pxster stores your username, email, password hash, login sessions, connected platform account identifiers and permissions, encrypted authorization tokens, uploaded media, captions, schedules, publishing results, and error history. Hosting providers may process connection and security logs.

How information is used and shared

This information enables authentication, account connection, media validation, scheduling, publishing, support, and abuse prevention. Content and required account information are sent to the social platforms you select. Cloudflare and the backend hosting provider process data to operate the service. Passwords and provider tokens are not exposed in the frontend.

Google and YouTube

When you connect YouTube, Pxster uses Google authorization to identify your channel and upload content you select. Google API data is used to provide these user-facing features, subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. See the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke access through your Google account permissions.

Cookies and security

Pxster uses an essential session cookie for login. Production sessions expire after seven days. Provider tokens are encrypted and passwords are hashed. Media delivery links grant temporary access to anyone holding the link; avoid sharing them.

Retention and deletion

Uploaded media and publishing history currently remain stored until manually removed; there is no automatic retention job. Disconnecting a platform removes its stored credentials from Pxster, but does not erase published posts or your account history. See data deletion instructions.

Before launch: the operator must specify retention periods, backup deletion handling, the backend hosting provider and processing locations, and the process for applicable privacy requests.

Your choices

You can disconnect platform accounts and revoke grants at the provider. Contact the operator for access, correction, or deletion requests. Do not send passwords or authorization tokens. Material changes to this policy should be communicated before they take effect.
